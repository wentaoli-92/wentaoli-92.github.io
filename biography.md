## Biography

Dr Wentao Li is currently a Lecturer at the University of Leicester since 2024. Before that, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) from 2023 to 2024, working under the supervision of Prof. [Wei Wang](https://facultyprofiles.hkust-gz.edu.cn/faculty-personal-page/WANG-Wei/weiwcs). Moreover, from 2020 to 2022, he held the position of postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) under the guidance of Prof. [Lu Qin](https://www.uts.edu.au/staff/lu.qin). He completed his Ph.D. study at UTS in 2021, advised by Prof. [Lu Qin](https://www.uts.edu.au/staff/lu.qin), Prof. [Ying Zhang](https://www.uts.edu.au/staff/ying.zhang), and Dr. [Miao Qiao](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/miao-qiao). He obtained his master's degree from Chongqing University in 2016 and his bachelor's degree from Nanchang Hangkong University in 2013. His research interests are in data processing and machine learning, with a particular focus on graph data processing and mining. To date, he has published more than 30 CORE A*/CCF-A level papers (including SIGMOD, VLDB, SIGKDD, ICDE, The Web Conference, TKDE, and VLDBJ), authored one monograph, and obtained one patent (generating 1.01 million RMB in business transformation revenue).

## Research Interests

His research interests primarily lie in large-scale data processing and machine learning. In particular, he is currently working on the following research topics:

- **Data Processing:** Graph Processing, Vector Databases, Social Network Analysis, and Parallel and Distributed Computing.
- **Machine Learning:** Graph Mining, Large Language Models (LLMs), Recommender Systems, and AI for Combinatorial Optimization.
